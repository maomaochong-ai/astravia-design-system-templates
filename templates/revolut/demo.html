Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Pure White (#ffffff) as the primary canvas
  • Revolut Blue (#0075EB) for all interactive elements
  • Charcoal (#191C1F) for all primary text
  • 12px radius for buttons, 16px for cards
  • 4px-based spacing with compact density
  • JetBrains Mono for financial data
  • Clean borders over heavy shadows
Don't — Avoid
  • Blue backgrounds on large surfaces
  • Multiple accent colors for interactions
  • Decorative gradients on functional elements
  • Border radius smaller than 8px
  • Heavy drop shadows
  • Display weights for body text
  • Mono fonts for non-data content
