Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • White Canvas (#ffffff) as primary background
  • Ink Black (#111111) for all primary text
  • Pin Red (#E60023) for CTAs and brand moments
  • Pill-shaped buttons (24px radius) for friendliness
  • 16px radius for pin cards with soft shadows
  • 4px-based spacing with generous breathing room
  • Let imagery dominate — keep chrome minimal
Don't — Avoid
  • Dark backgrounds for content areas
  • Pin Red for text — buttons and accents only
  • Hard sharp corners on cards or buttons
  • Heavy shadows or thick borders
  • Dense packing without breathing room
  • Font families outside system sans-serif
  • Monospace fonts for non-technical content
