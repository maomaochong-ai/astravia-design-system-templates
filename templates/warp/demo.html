Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do
  • Void Black (#000000) as primary background
  • Electric Cyan (#00D4FF) for active states and CTAs
  • Monospace for all terminal-facing content
  • Neon Green (#00ff88) for prompts and success
  • Compact spacing (12px gap) for density
  • 8px radius for terminal blocks, 6px for buttons
  • High contrast: white on black for primary text
Don't
  • Light backgrounds — the terminal is dark
  • Serif or decorative fonts in terminal contexts
  • Electric Cyan for large background fills
  • Low-contrast text (Dim White on Carbon)
  • Playful rounded shapes — keep it sharp
  • Mixing sans and mono in one terminal block
  • Heavy drop shadows — use borders or glows
