Guidelines

Use with restraint

Do

  • Use Acid Lime once per view.
  • Separate surfaces with Graphite hairlines.
  • Reserve Berkeley Mono for technical metadata.

Avoid

  • Do not use display weights above 590.
  • Do not add chromatic actions beyond Acid Lime.
  • Do not use outer shadows as general card elevation.
