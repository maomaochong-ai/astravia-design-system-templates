Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • #121212 as primary background — never pure black
  • Spotify Green exclusively for CTAs and active playback
  • Pill shapes (500px) for all buttons and chips
  • 8px radius for cards and containers
  • #B3B3B3 for secondary text, never primary
  • 16px minimum spacing between interactive elements
  • Let album art be the visual focal point
Don't — Avoid
  • Pure black (#000000) as background
  • Spotify Green for body text on dark backgrounds
  • Mixing pill buttons with sharp corners
  • Heavy drop shadows — use background shifts
  • More than 2 text colors in one view
  • Text on album art without overlays
  • Decorative borders — use #282828 shifts
