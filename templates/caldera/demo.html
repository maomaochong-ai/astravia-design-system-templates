06 / Direction

Usage rules

Concrete constraints drawn from the Caldera reference.

Preserve

  • Use Pumice as the broad page canvas.
  • Set structural headings at 48px or larger.
  • Keep DM Sans body copy at weight 500.
  • Reserve Ember for primary actions and key cards.

Avoid

  • Using Plasma Violet for controls or body text.
  • Adding extra chromatic accents beyond the three observed hues.
  • Compressing the 40px card padding.
  • Replacing compact display type with a generic sans.
