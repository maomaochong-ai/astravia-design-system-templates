Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • White (#ffffff) as primary canvas background
  • Shopify Green (#008060) for primary CTAs only
  • Text Primary (#1a1a1a) for all headings and body
  • Blue (#2c6ecb) for all links and secondary interactives
  • 8px radius for buttons, 12px for cards
  • 4px-based spacing with comfortable breathing room
  • Borders for structure over decorative shadows
Don't — Avoid
  • Shopify Green for text or non-primary elements
  • Red or orange for primary actions
  • Decorative shadows on cards
  • Hard sharp corners on buttons or cards
  • Dense packing without breathing room
  • Font families outside Inter / Inter Mono
  • Disabled text for actionable content
