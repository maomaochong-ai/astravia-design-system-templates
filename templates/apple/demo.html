Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Pure White (#ffffff) as the primary canvas
  • Jet Black (#1d1d1f) for all primary text
  • Apple Blue (#0071e3) exclusively for interactive elements
  • Whisper Gray (#f5f5f7) for section separation
  • Fully rounded (980px) pill buttons
  • 20px radius for cards and containers
  • Generous whitespace — 64px minimum between sections
  • Subtle shadows over hard borders
Don't — Avoid
  • Apple Blue for non-interactive decorative elements
  • Heavy drop shadows — keep them diffused and light
  • More than two text colors in a single block
  • Tight, cramped spacing without breathing room
  • Colored button backgrounds except Apple Blue for CTAs
  • Decorative or ornamental fonts
  • Hard-edged borders — prefer shadows or whitespace
  • Mono fonts for non-code content
