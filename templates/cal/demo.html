Use with restraint

Make the scheduling decision easier; leave everything else quiet.

Do

  • Use Cal Sans for headings and the UI face for reading text.
  • Let monochrome hierarchy, spacing and product surfaces do the work.
  • Use product fragments and integration marks as imagery.

Avoid

  • Gradients, broad decorative color and sharp corners.
  • Heavy borders on cards or type weights above 600.
  • Lifestyle imagery and display type for paragraph copy.
