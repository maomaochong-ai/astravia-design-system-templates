Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • White (#FFFFFF) as primary background
  • Brand Gradient for premium CTAs
  • Cardinal Red (#EB001B) for key brand moments
  • Midnight Blue (#1A1F71) for trust and links
  • 8px radius for buttons, 16px for cards
  • Compact spacing for financial data density
  • WCAG AA contrast on all text
Don't — Avoid
  • Cardinal Red for large background areas
  • Red and orange adjacent without gradient
  • Brand gradient for body text
  • Dark backgrounds for primary content
  • Dense text without 1.5 line-height
  • Mono fonts for non-financial content
  • Altering Mastercard circle proportions
