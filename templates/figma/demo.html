Guidelines

Use with restraint

Do

  • Reserve Electric Indigo for one CTA per viewport.
  • Use weight 320 display type at 56–72px.
  • Apply shadow-xl only to the hero card.

Avoid

  • Do not color ordinary UI controls with logo hues.
  • Do not shadow gallery cards.
  • Do not replace the white canvas with decorative surfaces.
