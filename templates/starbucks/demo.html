Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Parchment White (#FFFFFF) as primary canvas
  • Starbucks Green (#00704A) exclusively for CTAs
  • Espresso (#1E3932) for all primary text
  • Generous pill radius (50px buttons, 20px cards)
  • Cream Foam and Warm Linen for surface layering
  • Caramel (#C4975A) for decorative warm accents
  • Generous spacing with 4px grid, 16px+ gaps
Don't — Avoid
  • Pure black (#000000) for text — use Espresso
  • Harsh cool grays — use warm neutrals
  • Sharp corners on interactive elements
  • Starbucks Green for large background fills
  • Dense, cramped layouts without whitespace
  • Neon or saturated accent colors
  • Cherry Red outside of error states
