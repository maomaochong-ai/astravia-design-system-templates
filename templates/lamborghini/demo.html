Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Nero (#000000) as primary background — absolute black
  • Oro (#ddb321) exclusively for primary accents and CTAs
  • Uppercase display headings with tight negative tracking
  • 8px-based spacing with aggressive, tight density
  • Sharp border radii (0px) — no pills, no soft edges
  • Carbon weave texture on elevated surfaces
  • Rosso Mars (#cc0000) only for destructive actions
  • Gold–black contrast as the defining visual tension
Don't — Avoid
  • Light backgrounds — the system is dark-only
  • Rounded corners above 4px — they contradict the brand
  • Pastel or soft colors — the palette is high-contrast
  • Oro text on light backgrounds — must sit on dark
  • Decorative drop shadows — use surface color instead
  • Lowercase display headings — always uppercase
  • Multiple accent colors — gold is singular and dominant
  • Cramped hero sections — let the content breathe
