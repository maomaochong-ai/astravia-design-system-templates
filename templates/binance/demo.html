Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Obsidian (#0b0e11) as primary background
  • Bone White (#eaecef) for all primary text
  • Binance Yellow (#F0B90B) for primary CTAs only
  • Bull Green / Bear Red for directional data
  • Binance Mono for all prices and numbers
  • 4px border radius for sharp, precise UI
  • Compact spacing with clear row separation
Don't — Avoid
  • Binance Yellow for large backgrounds
  • Swapping Bull/Bear color semantics
  • Border radius larger than 8px
  • Decorative shadows or gradient glows
  • Proportional fonts for price data
  • Dense rows without separator borders
  • Font families outside the system
