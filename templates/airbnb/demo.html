Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • White (#ffffff) as primary canvas for content areas
  • Rausch (#FF5A5F) for primary CTAs and key interactive accents
  • Babu (#00A699) for secondary highlights and success states
  • Generous spacing — multiples of 8px, at least 24px card padding
  • 16px radius on cards, 8px on buttons for warm rounded feel
  • Large photography with breathing room — let images speak
  • Soft, diffused shadows (0 2px 16px rgba(0,0,0,.08))
Don't — Avoid
  • Rausch for backgrounds or large filled areas — accent only
  • Hard, sharp corners on any interactive element
  • Heavy, dark shadows — keep elevation subtle and airy
  • Crowded content — maintain generous whitespace
  • More than two accent colors (Rausch + Babu) per view
  • Monospace fonts for non-data content
  • Bright saturated colors that compete with Rausch
