Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • True Black (#000000) as primary background
  • Pure White (#ffffff) for all primary text and CTAs
  • Pill-shaped (9999px) buttons exclusively
  • Sharp 0px corners on cards, inputs, containers
  • Tight letter-spacing on headings (-0.02 to -0.04)
  • Uppercase with wide tracking for labels and nav
  • Maximum contrast at all times
  • Generous negative space — let content breathe
Don't — Avoid
  • Colored backgrounds — strictly black, white, grays
  • Rounded corners on non-button elements
  • Decorative shadows, gradients, or effects
  • Light or thin font weights for headings
  • Reduced contrast between text and background
  • Cluttered layouts — every element earns its place
  • Body copy in uppercase — reserve for labels only
  • Drop shadows on cards or elevated elements
