Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Void Black (#000000) as the primary canvas
  • Pure White (#ffffff) for all primary text
  • Vercel Blue (#0070f3) for links and accent CTAs
  • Gradient mesh sparingly for hero sections
  • 6px radius for buttons, 12px for cards
  • 4px-based compact spacing with ample whitespace
  • Border and contrast for elevation over shadows
Don't — Avoid
  • Colored backgrounds beyond the dark palette
  • Decorative shadows — use border contrast
  • Gradient mesh on small elements or body text
  • Dense layouts without breathing room
  • Font families beyond Geist / Inter
  • Vercel Blue for large background areas
  • Rounded corners larger than 12px on rectangles
