Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do - Embrace
  • Keep #FFFFFF as the uninterrupted page field
  • Use #0A0A0A only for text and decisive interaction
  • Give major statements 96-128px of vertical space
  • Separate panels and rows with #DBDBDB rules
  • Keep buttons at 9999px pill radius
  • Use Universal Sans with tight display tracking
  • Reserve Geist Mono for code and technical data
Don't - Avoid
  • Do not turn the page into a near-black theme
  • Do not crowd the centered hero with extra columns
  • Do not make every container a pill
  • Do not use monospace for long reading text
  • Do not replace muted navigation with loud accents
