06 / Direction

Usage rules

Color supports stories and proof; it does not replace the product hierarchy.

Preserve

  • Keep the large canvas white or soft cream.
  • Use black as the highest-priority action fill.
  • Give testimonial cards one solid color each.
  • Use 40px corners on large campaign cards.

Avoid

  • Turning Violet Pulse into a universal CTA fill.
  • Mixing several bright colors inside one card.
  • Adding depth to flat testimonial surfaces.
  • Using heavy display tracking outside the documented range.
