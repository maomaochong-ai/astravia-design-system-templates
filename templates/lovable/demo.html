Use the system quietly.

Do

  • Keep the hero gradient full-width and isolated.
  • Use Linen for every divider and visible border.
  • Keep headings at 480 and body copy at 400.
  • Use soft card and pill radii consistently.

Avoid

  • Cool grays, colored CTAs, and gradient text.
  • Additional type families.
  • Drop shadows outside the chat prompt.
  • Sharp corners on interactive controls.
