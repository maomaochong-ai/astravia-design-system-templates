Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Void Black (#0A0A0B) as the primary canvas
  • Ember (#FF6B00) exclusively for CTAs and links
  • Pure White (#ffffff) for all primary text
  • 6px radius for buttons, 12px for cards, 9999px for pills
  • 4px-based spacing with generous breathing room
  • Obsidian (#111113) for elevated surfaces
  • JetBrains Mono only for code and technical data
Don't — Avoid
  • Ember as a large surface background
  • Bright saturated backgrounds
  • Hard sharp corners on cards or buttons
  • Dense information packing without whitespace
  • Multiple accent colors for interactive elements
  • Monospace fonts for body copy or headings
  • Overly complex gradients or decorative shadows
