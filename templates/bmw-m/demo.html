06 / Direction

Guidelines

Preserve the system’s technical restraint: contrast, typography, and imagery carry the energy.

Do

  • Anchor primary layouts to the black canvas.
  • Keep display headlines concise, bold, and uppercase.
  • Use the M tricolor as a controlled brand signature.
  • Preserve documented spacing and interaction states.

Avoid

  • Introducing colors outside the documented palette.
  • Replacing white ink with an arbitrary neutral.
  • Softening every control with large corner radii.
  • Flattening spacing or component states for convenience.
