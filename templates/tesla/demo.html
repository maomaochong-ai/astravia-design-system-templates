Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Pure black (#000000) and white (#ffffff) as primary canvases
  • Ember Red (#e82127) exclusively for primary CTAs
  • Pill-shaped buttons (9999px radius) everywhere
  • Generous spacing — sections breathe with large padding
  • Tight letter-spacing on headings for premium feel
  • Full-bleed hero imagery at viewport scale
  • Subtle Graphite borders for minimal separation
  • Stark weight/size hierarchy over color variety
Don't — Avoid
  • Additional accent colors beyond red variants
  • Rounded corners >8px on non-button elements
  • Cramped, dense layouts without breathing room
  • Decoratory shadows — use flat color contrast
  • Playful or casual typography choices
  • Gradient backgrounds on primary surfaces
  • Bright saturated colors for secondary elements
  • Heavy dividers or visual noise
