Components

Button Variants

Primary (Wired Red), secondary (outlined), and ghost variants. All buttons use uppercase condensed bold with sharp editorial corners.

Primary Button
Main call to action — highest visual weight
.btn-primary
Wired Red (#ff0000) background, white text, 4px radius, uppercase condensed bold 14px. Use exclusively for the single most important action.
Secondary Button
Secondary actions, outlined editorial weight
.btn-secondary
Ink Black text, 1px Ink Black border, 4px radius. For secondary CTAs that need editorial weight without competing with primary.
Ghost Button
Tertiary actions, interactive accents
.btn-ghost
Electric Blue text, 1px Electric Blue border, 4px radius. For tertiary actions and interactive accent moments.

Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • White Canvas (#ffffff) as primary background
  • Ink Black (#000000) for headings and bold statements
  • Wired Red (#ff0000) exclusively for CTAs and badges
  • Electric Blue (#0066ff) for all links and focus states
  • Condensed Bold in all-caps for headlines
  • Generous whitespace with 48px+ section gaps
  • Sharp corners (0px) for editorial feel
Don't — Avoid
  • Wired Red for body text or large fills
  • Rounded corners (16px+) on primary elements
  • Mixing Condensed Bold with body text
  • Decorative gradients on text
  • Dense content packing without whitespace
  • Light Gray for primary text
  • Neon Yellow as a background color
