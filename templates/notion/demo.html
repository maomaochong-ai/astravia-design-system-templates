Guidance

Do’s & Don’ts

Do · Preserve

  • Use Paper Warmth (#f6f5f4) for the page and Pure White for cards.
  • Reserve Notion Blue (#0075de) for one primary action per view.
  • Use 1px rgba(0,0,0,.08) borders on content cards.
  • Set cards at 12px, buttons at 8px, and pills at 9999px.
  • Apply tight tracking to the 54px, 72px, and 96px display steps.
  • Rotate marigold, coral, sky, and midnight across flat feature panels.

Don’t · Dilute

  • Do not replace the warm canvas with a pure-white page.
  • Do not add shadows to ordinary content cards.
  • Do not introduce another chromatic filled-button color.
  • Do not render every text role at full black.
  • Do not use Lyon Text for navigation or product UI.
  • Do not use gradients or oversized rectangular radii.
