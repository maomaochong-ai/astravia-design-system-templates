Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do
  • Void Black (#000000) as primary background
  • NVIDIA Green (#76B900) exclusively for accents and CTAs
  • Platinum (#ffffff) for all primary text
  • Compact, information-dense layouts
  • Sharp geometric shapes and clean lines
  • NVIDIA Mono for all technical data and specs
  • Small border radii (4-12px) for precision
Don't
  • Use NVIDIA Green for large backgrounds
  • Introduce warm or saturated secondary colors
  • Use rounded, playful shapes
  • Apply generous, airy spacing
  • Add decorative illustrations or hand-drawn elements
  • Use script or display typefaces
  • Use mono fonts for non-code content
