06 / Direction

Usage rules

The Claude system gains personality from material warmth, typography, and restraint.

Preserve

  • Use Bone Parchment for large page backgrounds.
  • Set display headlines in the serif at weight 400.
  • Pair white cards with warm gray hairlines.
  • Keep Clay orange to marks and editorial accents.

Avoid

  • Using Clay orange as a button fill.
  • Introducing a second chromatic accent.
  • Using pure white as the full-page canvas.
  • Applying the serif to navigation or form controls.
