Keep the fire contained.

Do

  • Use Parchment as the full-page canvas.
  • Keep Ember to small directional accents.
  • Keep Arial at 400 with −0.025em tracking.
  • Use the warm four-layer shadow on dark product panels.

Avoid

  • Cool-gray shadows or extra chromatic accents.
  • Bold weight and Ember-filled CTAs.
  • Pill-shaped buttons and cards.
  • Dense multi-column card grids.
