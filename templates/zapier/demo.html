Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Clean White (#ffffff) as primary background
  • Midnight (#1a1a2e) for all primary text
  • Zapier Orange (#ff4a00) for CTAs and key actions
  • Green Success (#059669) for active states
  • 8px radius for buttons, 12px for cards
  • 4px-based spacing with friendly breathing room
  • Subtle shadows on hover for depth
Don't — Avoid
  • Orange for large background surfaces
  • More than two accent colors per view
  • Hard sharp corners on cards or buttons
  • Orange text on light orange backgrounds
  • Dense packing without breathing room
  • Font families outside DM Sans / Inter
  • Mono fonts for non-code content
