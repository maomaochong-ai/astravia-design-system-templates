Make the content speak.

Do

  • Use white canvas and Ink Black typography.
  • Reserve Cobalt Signal for promotional moments.
  • Keep display text left-aligned and tightly tracked.
  • Let research images remain full-bleed and sharp.

Avoid

  • Gradients, shadows and glow effects.
  • Secondary accent colors.
  • Centered long-form copy.
  • Rounded image cards or heavy controls.
