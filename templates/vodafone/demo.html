Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Cloud White (#ffffff) as primary background
  • Vodafone Red (#e60000) for CTAs and brand moments
  • Charcoal (#333333) for all body text
  • Night (#1a1a1a) only for strongest emphasis
  • 8px radius for buttons, 16px for cards
  • 4px-based spacing with generous breathing room
  • Snow (#f7f7f7) for alternate section backgrounds
Don't — Avoid
  • Vodafone Red for large background areas
  • Red text on dark backgrounds without contrast check
  • Mixing red shades in a single interactive element
  • Amber or Signal Red outside warning/error contexts
  • Dense, tight spacing without breathing room
  • Decorative gradients on small UI elements
  • Monospace font for non-technical content
