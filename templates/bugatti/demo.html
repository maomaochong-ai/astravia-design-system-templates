Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Absolute Black (#000000) as primary canvas
  • Bugatti Blue (#0047AB) for interactive elements
  • Heritage Gold (#C5A55A) for premium highlights only
  • EB Garamond for all display headlines
  • Generous whitespace — spacious, unhurried layouts
  • 2px radius for buttons, 4px for cards
  • Uppercase labels with 0.06px letter-spacing
Don't — Avoid
  • Bright or saturated colors outside the palette
  • Rounded corners above 12px
  • Decorative drop shadows
  • Dense, cluttered layouts
  • Mixing serif and sans-serif in same hierarchy
  • Heritage Gold for body text or large blocks
  • Casual or playful UI copy
