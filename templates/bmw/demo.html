Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Alpine White / Night Black as primary backgrounds
  • BMW Blue exclusively for CTAs, links, and interactive elements
  • 8px grid with generous 32px section gaps
  • Weight 300 for hero text, 600 for headings
  • 4px radius for buttons, 8px for cards — precise edges
  • Subtle shadows (0 2px 8px) over heavy elevation
  • Gold Accent sparingly for M Sport or premium tiers
Don't — Avoid
  • BMW Blue for large background fills
  • Border radius larger than 8px
  • Heavy decorative drop shadows
  • Signal colors for non-semantic purposes
  • Tight spacing without breathing room
  • More than two font families per view
  • BMW Blue text on BMW Blue backgrounds
