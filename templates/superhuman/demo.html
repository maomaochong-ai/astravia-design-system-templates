Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Pure White (#ffffff) as the primary canvas
  • Jet Black (#000000) for bold headings and CTAs
  • Electric Blue (#2563eb) for all links and focus states
  • Compact 4px-based spacing for dense email lists
  • Inter weights 400–700; 700 for critical headings only
  • Keyboard badges (JetBrains Mono) beside actions
  • 6px buttons, 8px inputs, 12px cards — consistent radius
Don't — Avoid
  • Colored backgrounds behind email content
  • Decorative gradients or illustrations in the inbox
  • Font sizes below 11px for readable text
  • More than one accent color for interactions
  • Heavy drop shadows — use none or subtle only
  • Radius sharper than 4px — feels unfinished
  • Jet Black text on dark backgrounds
